GIOI THIEU HE THONG FUNDAS - TANG INFERENCE (FastAPI) VA TANG WEB (Next.js)
============================================================================
(Kiem tra truc tiep tu code + config.json thuc te ngay 2026-09-20, khong doan)


PHAN 1: TANG INFERENCE (FastAPI, port 8000, inference/main.py)
==================================================================
Mot tien trinh Python duy nhat, giu 7 model thuong tru trong VRAM (~5GB/8GB
GPU), load 1 lan luc khoi dong (~60-90s), tai su dung cho moi request.
Khong luu tru gi (khong DB, khong Cloudinary) -- chi nhan anh, tra ket qua.
Toan bo 7 model deu la seresnext50_32x4d hoac tuong duong (rieng DR
ensemble dung 4 backbone khac nhau).


1. DR ENSEMBLE (model chinh, "Fundas · Cham diem vong mac")
--------------------------------------------------------------
  - Nhiem vu: Cham do Diabetic Retinopathy (Vong mac tieu duong), 5 muc do
    (0=Khong benh -> 4=Rat nang, "Proliferative DR").
  - Kien truc: ENSEMBLE 4 backbone (inception_v4, inception_resnet_v2,
    seresnext50_32x4d, seresnext101_32x4d) x 5 fold moi backbone = 20 model
    con, ket hop bang weighted average + threshold toi uu theo QWK
    (Quadratic Weighted Kappa).
  - Day la model duy nhat co do phuc tap/quy mo train lon nhat he thong,
    va la model DUY NHAT co san Grad-CAM explain (endpoint /explain) --
    ve heatmap vung anh huong den quyet dinh cua model.
  - Endpoint: POST /predict (cham diem), POST /explain (heatmap PNG base64).
  - Trang thai: San pham chinh, khong ghi "is_experimental" trong response
    (khac voi 5 model chuyen biet ben duoi, tat ca deu la "specialist
    screen" bo sung, co disclaimer "experimental").


2. CLIP MODALITY GATE (lop gac ngoai cung, khong phai model chan doan)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Nhiem vu: KHONG cham benh -- chi kiem tra loai anh co dung khong TRUOC
    khi cho vao bat ky model nao khac. Vi du: anh chup mat ngoai gui nham
    vao /predict (dang cho anh day mat) se bi chan o day, khong bao gio
    toi duoc DR ensemble.
  - Ky thuat: CLIP zero-shot (open_clip, ViT-B-32/openai, KHONG train rieng
    -- chi so sanh embedding anh voi vai cau mo ta van ban cho 3 nhom:
    "fundus" (day mat), "external_eye" (mat ngoai), "other" (khong lien
    quan mat).
  - Ap dung cho CA 7 endpoint nhan anh: 6 endpoint doi anh fundus
    (predict/explain/quality-check/multidisease/glaucoma/amd) va 2 endpoint
    cataract (crop-preview + screen, doi anh mat ngoai).
  - Chan cung (HTTP 422) neu sai loai anh, khong co canh bao mem.
  - Chua duoc calibrate bang bo du lieu nhan tay chinh thuc -- chi test
    tren vai chuc anh that (xem config/modality_gate/config.json).


3. QUALITY GATEKEEPER -- "QuickQual" (kiem tra chat luong anh)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Nhiem vu: Danh gia anh fundus co du "Tot / Dung duoc / Kem" de doc hay
    khong (dua vao do net, anh sang, che khuat...).
  - Ky thuat: DenseNet121 (dong bang, chi trich xuat dac trung) + bo phan
    loai SVM co san cua tac gia goc (khong tu train), phuong phap QuickQual
    (Engelmann & MacGillivray, 2023).
  - Endpoint rieng: POST /quality-check (web goi rieng de LUU nhan chat
    luong vao DB, khong chan gi ca -- gate_mode=fail_open).
  - Cung duoc nhung (embedded) vao 5 endpoint fundus khac de LOG canh bao
    server-side neu chat luong kem -- nhung KHONG con chan cung nua (da bo
    theo quyet dinh 2026-09-06: qua nhay, tung chan oan mot anh DR that
    hop le; viec danh gia chat luong du de doc hay khong nen de bac si
    quyet dinh, khong phai automation).
  - License: tac gia goc khong cong bo license ro rang -- CHI dung ca nhan/
    phi thuong mai, khong duoc phan phoi lai.


4. MULTI-DISEASE SCREENING (sang loc 8 nhom benh)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Nhiem vu: Sang loc dong thoi 8 nhom: normal, diabetes, glaucoma,
    cataract, amd, hypertension, myopia, other -- tu MOT anh fundus.
  - Dataset: ODIR-5K (Kaggle, andrewmvd/ocular-disease-recognition-odir5k).
  - Do chinh xac: val_accuracy = 62.2%, val_macro_f1 = 58.1% -- THAP hon
    han DR ensemble, la bo phan loai single-label 8-lop (khong phai
    multi-label du mot mat co the co nhieu benh cung luc).
  - Endpoint: POST /multidisease-screen. Tinh nang doc lap, khong tu dong
    chay chung voi /predict -- nguoi dung phai bam chon rieng.
  - Trang thai: "is_experimental": true, ro rang la sang loc bo sung THAM
    KHAO, khong phai ket qua chan doan.


5. GLAUCOMA SPECIALIST (chuyen sau ve Tang nhan ap/Glaucoma)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Nhiem vu: Phan loai nhi phan normal/glaucoma, chuyen sau hon multi-
    disease cho benh nay.
  - Dataset: gop 3 nguon (ACRIMA, ORIGA, RIM-ONE), toan la anh DA CROP SAN
    quanh dia thi giac (optic disc) -- KHONG phai anh fundus toan canh.
  - Vi vay inference/glaucoma_engine.py phai TU DONG dinh vi + crop vung
    dia thi giac truoc khi cham (dung fundus_image_toolbox, model
    efficientnet-b3 rieng tai ve tu Zenodo, cache local o
    ~/AppData/Local/fundus_image_toolbox).
  - Do chinh xac tren anh da crop san (val, khong thuc te): f1=87.3%.
  - Do chinh xac THUC TE end-to-end (anh fundus toan canh that + tu dong
    crop): accuracy=77.0%, f1=77.7% -- do bang scripts/validate_glaucoma_e2e.py
    tren 770 anh that, GIAM ~10 diem so voi so lieu val -- day la chi phi
    that cua buoc tu dong crop, da duoc do that, khong phai doan.
  - Tham so crop (od_crop_fraction=0.20) da duoc sweep thu nhieu gia tri
    tren tap validation rieng de chon toi uu, khong phai chon boi mat.
  - Endpoint: POST /glaucoma-screen. Doc lap, co the goi tu ket qua
    multi-disease (nut "Phan tich chuyen sau") hoac chay rieng.


6. AMD SPECIALIST (Thoai hoa diem vang)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Nhiem vu: Phan loai nhi phan normal/amd.
  - Dataset: AMDNet23 (~900 anh, gop 6 nguon nho).
  - Tien xu ly: Gamma correction (1.2) + CLAHE truoc khi cham, de khop voi
    cach anh training da duoc xu ly.
  - Do chinh xac val: 99.1% f1 -- nhung CANH BAO RO trong chinh
    config/amd/config.json: day la dau hieu OVERFITTING kinh dien (train
    loss ve gan 0 tu epoch 12, val_recall dinh o 1.0 suot 18/25 epoch, tren
    tap du lieu nho/nhieu nguon trung lap tiem an). SO NAY GAN NHU CHAC
    CHAN THOI PHONG, khong phan anh do chinh xac thuc te.
  - Day la model DUY NHAT trong 6 model chuyen biet CHUA duoc validate
    end-to-end tren du lieu doc lap (khac glaucoma da co e2e that) -- ly do:
    khong tim duoc bo du lieu AMD nao rieng biet, khong trung ODIR-5K.
  - Endpoint: POST /amd-screen. Trien khai theo quyet dinh ro rang cua
    nguoi dung du chua du tin cay cao, vi day khong phai tinh nang uu tien.


7. CATARACT SPECIALIST (Duc thuy tinh the -- model DUY NHAT khong dung anh
   fundus)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Nhiem vu: Phan loai nhi phan normal/cataract, TU ANH CHUP MAT NGOAI
    (nhu anh selfie can canh), khac hoan toan 6 model con lai (tat ca deu
    doi anh soi day mat).
  - Dataset: Kaggle orvile/eye-diseases-classification, anh can canh 1 mat.
  - Buoc rieng: Tu dong nhan dien khuon mat + khoanh vung mat bang YuNet
    (cv2.FaceDetectorYN, KHONG phai Haar cascade -- ban opencv-python cai
    trong may nay (5.0.0) da bo cv2.CascadeClassifier).
  - Luong "xac nhan crop truoc khi phan tich" (xay dung 2026-09-06):
      1) POST /cataract-crop-preview -- chi crop, tra anh da crop (PNG
         base64) cho web hien thi, CHUA phan loai.
      2) Nguoi dung xac nhan tren giao dien -> POST /cataract-screen voi
         co already_cropped=true -- phan loai TRUC TIEP tren anh da duoc
         duyet, KHONG chay lai YuNet lan 2 (tranh 2 lan detect ra 2 vung
         khac nhau).
  - Do chinh xac val: 100% (f1=1.0) -- nhung config.json ghi ro day la
    "yellow flag": phien ban dau tien (v1) bi loi shortcut-learning (can
    bang sai theo do phan giai pixel thay vi do net thuc te), sua lai (v2,
    dang dung) giam duoc ty le chenh lech do net giua 2 lop tu 4.45x xuong
    2.10x nhung CHUA VE HET 1x -- van con nghi van residual.
  - Gioi han quan trong CHUA giai duoc: buoc lam mo anh "normal" luc train
    (de sua shortcut) la bien doi theo NHAN, khong the lam lai luc du doan
    that (vi nhan la thu dang can du doan) -- anh that mo tu nhien co the
    bi lech phan phoi so voi luc train.
  - Endpoint: POST /cataract-crop-preview, POST /cataract-screen.
  - AN TOAN LUONG (2026-09-06): them threading.Lock() quanh YuNet detector
    dung chung -- nghi van (chua xac nhan) day la nguyen nhan 1 lan crash
    toan tien trinh khong co traceback, do cv2.dnn Net co the khong an toan
    khi nhieu request goi dong thoi.

PHAN 2: TANG WEB (Next.js 16 App Router, port 3000, web/)
==================================================================
Khong goi truc tiep tang inference tu trinh duyet -- moi request di qua
API route cua chinh Next.js (web/src/app/api/*), route do moi goi sang
FastAPI (INFERENCE_URL). Luu du lieu vao Postgres (Neon, qua Prisma) +
anh vao Cloudinary (chi khi da dang nhap).


A. TRANG DANH CHO BENH NHAN (patient-facing)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
  /upload         - Trang chinh: cham DR (goi /predict + /quality-check
                    song song), luu thanh 1 "Scan" neu da dang nhap.
  /multidisease   - Sang loc 8 nhom benh (co the tai su dung anh tu 1 Scan
                    da co, khong bat upload lai).
  /glaucoma       - Chuyen sau Glaucoma (co the goi tu ket qua multidisease
                    hoac chay doc lap).
  /amd            - Chuyen sau AMD, panel ket qua rieng theo nguong xac
                    suat (5 bac mau sev-0..sev-4), khong hien phan tram
                    tho (da biet la thoi phong).
  /cataract       - Duy nhat KHONG dung anh fundus. Ho tro 2 cach dua anh
                    vao: tai file HOAC mo camera thiet bi chup truc tiep
                    (getUserMedia, xay dung 2026-09-04) -- sau do BAT BUOC
                    qua man hinh "xac nhan vung mat da khoanh" truoc khi
                    moi thuc su phan tich (xay dung 2026-09-06).
  /history        - Lich su tat ca lan sang loc cua nguoi dung (Scan +
                    4 loai Screening).
  /account        - Thong tin ca nhan.
  /login, /register - Dang nhap/dang ky. Khach (chua dang nhap) van dung
                    duoc moi tinh nang sang loc, chi khong duoc LUU lai
                    (AnonymousSaveNudge nhac dang ky de luu).
  /results/[imageId] - Xem lai 1 Scan da luu.


B. TRANG DANH CHO BAC SI (doctor-facing, /doctor/*)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
  /doctor              - "Ban kham", trang tong quan.
  /doctor/queue         - "Hang doi" cac ca can xu ly.
  /doctor/patients      - Danh sach + ho so tung benh nhan (/[id]).
  /doctor/new-case      - Bac si tao ho so benh nhan vang lai (khong can
                    dang nhap cua ho) + Scan dau tien trong 1 request.
  /doctor/case/[id]     - Xem/chot danh gia 1 ca DR (DoctorReview).
  /doctor/screening/[id] - Xem/chot danh gia 1 ca multi-disease
                    (ScreeningReview).
  /doctor/glaucoma/[id], /doctor/cataract/[id] - Xem/chot danh gia rieng
                    cho tung loai (GlaucomaReview, CataractReview) --
                    dong vong lap bac si <-> benh nhan cho tung chuyen khoa.
  /doctor/profile       - Ho so ca nhan bac si (CV, chuyen khoa...).
  Luu y: chua co phan quyen role=="doctor" thuc su -- bat ky session dang
  nhap nao cung dung duoc cac trang nay (giai doan 1, ghi ro trong code).


C. CAU TRUC DU LIEU CHINH (Prisma, Postgres)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
  User                  - Tai khoan (benh nhan/bac si dung chung 1 bang,
                    phan biet qua field "role"). Email/mat khau la optional
                    (ho tro benh nhan vang lai do bac si tao, khong dang
                    nhap duoc).
  Scan                  - 1 lan cham DR (grade, score, qualityLabel...).
  MultidiseaseScreening, GlaucomaScreening, AmdScreening, CataractScreening
                    - Moi loai sang loc chuyen biet co bang RIENG (khong
                    gop chung voi Scan) -- vi moi model co canh bao/do tin
                    cay khac nhau, khong duoc de lam nguoi dung nham la
                    "cung muc do dang tin" voi Scan.grade.
  DoctorReview, ScreeningReview, GlaucomaReview, CataractReview - Danh gia
                    cua bac si cho tung loai sang loc tuong ung, 1-1 voi
                    ban ghi goc, LUON la bo sung ben canh ket qua AI, khong
                    bao gio ghi de len.


D. DANH SACH API ROUTE (web/src/app/api/*)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
  /api/scans                - POST tao Scan moi (cham DR + quality-check).
  /api/multidisease          - POST sang loc 8 nhom (nhan file MOI hoac
                    scanId de dung lai anh cu).
  /api/glaucoma               - POST chuyen sau glaucoma (nhan file MOI
                    hoac multidiseaseScreeningId).
  /api/amd                    - POST chuyen sau AMD.
  /api/cataract               - POST phan loai cataract (ho tro co
                    alreadyCropped/eyeCropApplied cho luong xac nhan crop).
  /api/cataract/crop-preview  - POST rieng, chi tra ve anh da crop de xac
                    nhan, chua phan loai.
  /api/doctor/*               - Cac thao tac phia bac si (tao ca moi, xem
                    hang doi, chot danh gia...).
  /api/auth                   - Dang nhap/dang ky/dang xuat.
  Tat ca route nhan file upload deu co gioi han 20MB rieng (khop voi tang
  inference), chan ngay tu header Content-Length truoc khi doc form data
  (them 2026-09-06, vi ban vá ben inference KHONG bao ve duoc tang web).
