2. VIEC CAN KIEM TRA SAU KHI TAI ANH THAT (chua the xac nhan tu metadata)
----------------------------------------------------------
    Day la nhung rui ro ky thuat CHUA THE loai tru neu chi doc mo ta --
    phai tai vai chuc anh mau ve kiem tra truc tiep TRUOC khi cam ket huan
    luyen toan bo, giong cach da phat hien loi nhan per-eye/patient-level o
    ODIR-5K (plan.txt Phase 2) chi sau khi mo file that.

    2.1 Quy uoc crop CO THE KHAC NHAU giua 3 bo -- rui ro lon nhat
        Mo ta ACRIMA noi ro anh "were cropped around the optic disc and
        renamed" -- tuc la ACRIMA CO THE la anh da crop sat quanh dia thi
        giac (mot vung nho cua fundus), KHAC HAN voi anh fundus TRON DAY DU
        (ca vong mac) ma inference/preprocessing.py::load_and_crop() dang
        gia dinh (crop vien den quanh HINH TRON DAY MAT chuan APTOS-kernel).
        Neu ACRIMA thuc su la anh da-crop-san quanh dia thi giac, chay
        load_and_crop len no se cho ket qua sai (khong con vien den de
        crop, hoac crop nham). ORIGA/RIM-ONE nhieu kha nang la anh fundus
        day du (giong DR) nhung CAN XAC NHAN TRUC TIEP, khong duoc gia dinh.
        => Hanh dong: tai ~10-20 anh mau tu MOI bo, mo bang mat / hien thi
        kich thuoc-ty le, so sanh voi anh fundus DR hien co trong test_
        images/ TRUOC khi quyet dinh dung chung preprocessing.py hay can
        viet 1 nhanh preprocessing rieng cho ACRIMA (vd bo qua load_and_crop,
        chi resize+normalize thang).

    2.2 Khong co patient ID trong ACRIMA/ORIGA/RIM-ONE (khac ODIR-5K)
        ODIR-5K co cot patient ID ro rang de split tranh ro ri du lieu
        (2 mat cung 1 nguoi). Mo ta 3 bo glaucoma o tren KHONG de cap patient
        ID -- ACRIMA chi co so thu tu anh (Im001..Im705), khong ro co phai
        moi anh 1 benh nhan khac nhau hay khong (co the co benh nhan xuat
        hien ca 2 mat/nhieu lan chup). Can kiem tra file/thu muc that xem co
        metadata nao (ten file, CSV di kem) tiet lo quan he benh nhan-anh
        hay khong; neu khong co, chi con cach chap nhan split theo tung anh
        (rui ro ro ri nho hon ODIR vi tap nho hon nhung van nen ghi nhan ro
        trong code/doc, khong gia vo la split-theo-benh-nhan).

    2.3 Domain shift giua 3 nguon (Tay Ban Nha, Singapore, Tay Ban Nha)
        ACRIMA + RIM-ONE deu tu benh vien Tay Ban Nha (nhung khac benh
        vien/may chup), ORIGA tu Singapore (nghien cuu SiMES, dan so Malay).
        May chup, anh sang, dan so benh nhan khac nhau giua 3 nguon co the
        gay model hoc "dau hieu nguon goc anh" thay vi "dau hieu glaucoma
        that" (shortcut learning). Khuyen nghi: luon stratify split theo CA
        (label, nguon-dataset) -- khong chi theo label -- de moi nguon deu
        co mat can doi trong ca train/val, va bao cao rieng accuracy theo
        tung nguon (khong chi accuracy tong) de phat hien neu model chi hoc
        tot 1 nguon.


3. DE XUAT KIEN TRUC/HUAN LUYEN (dua kinh nghiem Phase 2 ODIR-5K)
----------------------------------------------------------
    - KHONG dung ensemble "4 backbone x 5 fold = 20 model" nhu DR ngay tu
      dau -- tap hop 1840 anh qua nho de train 20 model rieng khong overfit
      nang. Bat dau giong Phase 2: 1 backbone don (co the tai dung
      seresnext50_32x4d cho nhat quan) + 1 head Linear/sigmoid nhi phan.
    - Vi tap nho (~1840 anh, nho hon nhieu so 14.4K cua ODIR-5K), 1 lan
      split 85/15 co dinh se cho tap validation chi ~276 anh -- do nhieu
      thong ke kha lon giua cac epoch. Nen dung K-fold cross-validation
      (vd 5-fold) thay vi 1 split co dinh, de co uoc luong on dinh hon
      truoc khi quyet dinh co "dat yeu cau" hay khong.
    - Loss: co the dung BCE (nhi phan) thay vi CrossEntropy 8-lop nhu Phase
      2 -- don gian hon vi day chi la 2 lop (glaucoma/normal), khong can
      class-weight manh vi ty le 40/60 kha can bang (khac 22 lan lech cua
      ODIR-5K).
    - Preprocessing: TUY THUOC ket qua kiem tra muc 2.1 -- neu ACRIMA thuc
      su la anh da-crop-quanh-dia-thi-giac, co the can 2 nhanh xu ly khac
      nhau trong cung 1 script train (mot cho anh fundus day du, mot cho
      anh da crop san) hoac loai ACRIMA khoi tap train chinh va chi dung
      lam tap test rieng.
    - Ha tang: dua tren kinh nghiem Phase 2 (train local bi loi buffer +
      tranh chap CPU voi 1 tien trinh Python he thong chay nham, phai huy
      ca hai va chuyen sang Colab) -- khuyen nghi DUNG LAI pipeline Colab
      (plans/ggcolab.txt) ngay tu dau thay vi thu local truoc, tranh lap
      lai su co da gap. Tap du lieu nho hon nhieu (1840 vs 14.4K anh) nen
      thoi gian train/tai se nhanh hon dang ke.
