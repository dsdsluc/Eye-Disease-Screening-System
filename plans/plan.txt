FUNDAS_SYSTEM -- ROADMAP MO RONG NGOAI DR GRADING
====================================================
Ghi lai ket qua nghien cuu (2026-08-29) ve cac model/dataset benh mat cong khai
co the bo sung vao he thong, de Fundas_System khong con gioi han o cham diem
vong mac tieu duong (DR) ma tro thanh nen tang sang loc mat da benh.

Boi canh: he thong hien tai (xem plans/archetechture.txt) chi lam 1 task duy
nhat -- DR grading tu 1 anh day (fundus) 224x224, qua ensemble 4 backbone
(inception_v4, inception_resnet_v2, seresnext50_32x4d, seresnext101_32x4d) x
5 fold = 20 model timm, cong voi trong so hoc duoc.


1. SHORTLIST UU TIEN (gia tri/cong suc tich hop tot nhat)
----------------------------------------------------------
(1) Danh gia chat luong anh fundus (image quality / gradability) -- GATEKEEPER
    - Ly do uu tien cao nhat: rui ro thap, tan dung 100% pipeline san co
      (load_and_crop, preprocess_for_backbone khong doi).
    - Chi can 1 backbone nhe (khong can ensemble 20 model), phan loai
      Good/Usable/Reject truoc khi dua anh vao pipeline DR.
    - Dataset: EyeQ (tap con EyePACS gan nhan lai, ~28.8k anh, 3 lop).
    - Model tham khao: FundusQ-Net (regression), FGR-Net
      (github.com/saifalkhaldiurv/FGR_Net).

(2) Sang loc Glaucoma (phien ban phan loai, KHONG phai segmentation CDR)
    - Cung mau kien truc voi DR hien tai: 1 anh fundus -> 1 diem so/nguong
      -> nhan glaucoma/khong. Co the tai su dung nguyen class DRModel voi
      head moi.
    - Dataset cong khai: ORIGA (650 anh), ACRIMA (705 anh), RIM-ONE,
      REFUGE/REFUGE2 (1200-2000 anh, qua IEEE DataPort / grand-challenge.org).
    - Phien ban CDR (cup-to-disc ratio) can segmentation (U-Net) -- phuc tap
      hon nhieu, xep sau trong roadmap (xem muc 2.6).

(3) Phan loai da benh tren 1 anh (multi-label) -- ODIR-5K hoac RFMiD
    - Gia tri lon nhat tren moi don vi cong suc: 1 lan train/fine-tune moi
      backbone them 1 head multi-label (sigmoid) la phu duoc nhieu benh
      cung luc (glaucoma, AMD, dau hieu duc thuy tinh the, tang huyet ap
      vong mac, DR...) thay vi 20 model rieng cho tung benh.
    - ODIR-5K: 5.000 benh nhan, cap mat trai/phai, 8 nhan (Beijing
      Ophthalmology and Optometry Institute).
    - RFMiD: 3.200 anh, 46 benh, 3 loai may chup (Kaggle).
    - Day la huong "tan dung kien truc hien tai gan nhu nguyen ven" ro nhat:
      cung input 224x224, cung ho backbone timm, chi doi head cuoi.

(4) Phan do AMD (Age-related Macular Degeneration)
    - Thang do muc do tuong tu DR (ordinal 0-4 hoac 9-step AREDS scale) --
      gan giong bai toan DR nhat ve mat ky thuat.
    - Rao can chinh: dataset chuan (AREDS, 59.302 anh) bi khoa quyen truy
      cap qua dbGaP (phai xin phep). Thay the mo hon: HYAMD (PhysioNet).
    - Model tham khao: DeepSeeNet (arxiv.org/pdf/1811.07492), ensemble CNN,
      QWK ~0.92 tren phan loai 13 lop.


2. DANH SACH DAY DU CAC UNG VIEN DA KHAO SAT
----------------------------------------------------------
2.1 Danh gia chat luong anh (gatekeeper)
    Modality: fundus (giong anh dau vao hien tai)
    Dataset/model: EyeQ; FundusQ-Net; FGR-Net (github)
    Do phuc tap: CNN nho (ResNet/EfficientNet), 3 lop hoac 1 head regression
    Ghi chu tich hop: chay TRUOC buoc load_and_crop (tren anh goc chua crop),
    hau nhu khong doi preprocessing.

2.2 Sang loc Glaucoma (phan loai)
    Modality: fundus
    Dataset: ORIGA, ACRIMA, RIM-ONE, DRISHTI-GS1, REFUGE/REFUGE2
    Do phuc tap: CNN phan loai nhi phan -- fit gan nhu nguyen ven kien truc
    DR hien tai (regression/threshold -> grade).
    Ghi chu: neu lam theo huong segmentation dia thi/cup (CDR >= 0.65 la
    dau hieu) thi can kien truc khac han (U-Net), xem muc 2.6.

2.3 Phan do AMD
    Modality: fundus
    Dataset: AREDS (khoa quyen, dbGaP), HYAMD (PhysioNet, mo)
    Model: DeepSeeNet (ensemble CNN)
    Do phuc tap: tuong tu DR grading (ordinal severity regression)
    Ghi chu: gan nhat ve mat ky thuat voi bai toan DR dang co, nhung dataset
    kho xin hon.

2.4 Duc thuy tinh the (Cataract) -- CANH BAO MODALITY KHAC
    Modality: anh den khe (slit-lamp) / anh phan chieu vong (retro-
    illumination) -- KHONG PHAI anh fundus tieu chuan dang dung.
    Dataset: bo du lieu slit-lamp (1.335 slit-lamp + 637 retro-illumination,
    cham diem LOCS III), ensemble ResNet18/WideResNet50-2/ResNeXt50.
    Do phuc tap: ensemble ResNet/ResNeXt -- kien truc gan giong hien tai,
    nhung INPUT KHAC (can may chup slit-lamp va luong upload/UI rieng).
    Phuong an fundus-proxy (dung do mo anh de doan duc thuy tinh the) co
    ton tai nhung do tin cay thap hon, nen chi dung nhu "flag" tham khao,
    khong phai chan doan chinh.

2.5 Vong mac do tang huyet ap (Hypertensive retinopathy)
    Modality: fundus
    Dataset: BRSET (16.266 anh, multi-label, Brazil), OIA-ODIR, CGI-HRDC
    Model: Swin-L ViT (AUC 0.81/F1 0.97 tren BRSET) hoac MA-DNet (DenseNet,
    95.8% acc tren OIA-ODIR)
    Ghi chu: fit truc tiep vao mau phan loai hien tai nhung dataset chuyen
    biet con it/yeu hon glaucoma/AMD -- nen lam nhu 1 head trong model
    multi-label (muc 1.3) thay vi model rieng.

2.6 Phan doan mach mau vong mac (Vessel segmentation) -- TASK HO TRO
    Modality: fundus
    Dataset: DRIVE, CHASE_DB1, STARE (bo nho, kinh dien)
    Model co san trong luong: SA-UNet (github.com/clguo/SA-UNet),
    SGL-Retinal-Vessel-Segmentation (github.com/SHI-Labs/..., SOTA
    DRIVE/CHASE, co pretrained)
    Do phuc tap: U-Net (khac han timm classifier hien tai) -- can class
    model moi + output dang mask, khong phai diem so.
    Ghi chu: nen dung nhu buoc ho tro/QA (vd kiem tra do net mach mau lam
    dau vao cho CDR) hon la 1 tinh nang chan doan doc lap hien thi cho
    nguoi dung cuoi.

2.7 Foundation model thay the (huong dai han)
    Modality: fundus VA OCT
    Model: RETFound (github.com/rmaphoh/RETFound) -- ViT-Large pretrain
    tu-giam-sat (MAE/DINOv2) tren 1.6 trieu anh vong mac, weight co san tren
    HuggingFace, fine-tune duoc cho nhieu task ha nguon.
    Do phuc tap: ViT-Large -- nang hon 4 backbone CNN dang dung.
    Ghi chu: thay vi them 1 backbone CNN + 20 model moi cho MOI benh, co the
    chuyen dan sang 1 encoder nen dung chung + head nhe cho tung task --
    mo duong ho tro ca OCT sau nay ma khong can pipeline train tu dau lan 2.


3. TAC DONG KIEN TRUC (xem chi tiet o plans/archetechture.txt muc 9)
----------------------------------------------------------
- Nhom "fit gan nhu nguyen ven" (phan loai/regression 1 anh 224x224): image
  quality, glaucoma (ban phan loai), AMD, hypertensive retinopathy, va
  multi-label ODIR/RFMiD -- deu dung chung load_and_crop/
  preprocess_for_backbone va mau "N backbone x N fold + trong so ensemble"
  dang co. Toi uu vmap-batched-forward (xem archetechture.txt muc 7.2) ap
  dung duoc ngay cho bat ky task moi nao theo mau nay.

- Nhom "can nang luc moi" (segmentation): glaucoma-qua-CDR va vessel
  segmentation can kien truc U-Net + output dang mask, khac han
  DRModel/regressor hien tai -- day la khoi luong cong viec lon hon han so
  voi them 1 task phan loai.

- Modality khac (cataract that su, qua slit-lamp): KHONG the gan vao luong
  "upload 1 anh fundus" hien tai ma khong lam nguoi dung hieu nham anh can
  chup. Can luong capture/UI rieng neu lam that.

- Canh bao ve VRAM/scale: neu moi benh moi lai nhan ban kieu "4 backbone x
  5 fold = 20 model rieng" nhu DR hien tai, VRAM se tang tuyen tinh theo so
  benh (hien da dung gan het ~7.4GB cho rieng DR). Huong ben vung hon la (a)
  train 1 model multi-label dung chung backbone cho nhieu benh cung luc
  (muc 1.3/2.5), hoac (b) chuyen dan sang 1 foundation-model encoder dung
  chung (muc 2.7) voi head nhe rieng cho tung task, thay vi nhan N x 20
  model doc lap.
