3.1 AMDNet23 / "Macular Degeneration Disease Dataset"
    Kaggle ref: orvile/macular-degeneration-disease-dataset
    Nguon goc that: Mendeley Data (Ali, 2025), doi: 10.17632/yj35kjgrv3.1
    License: CC BY 4.0 (ro rang, tot)
    Mo ta: 2.000 anh, 4 lop can bang (Normal/Diabetes/Cataract/AMD, 400
    anh/lop), "compiled from six public sources: Ocular Disease
    Recognition [[= ODIR]], DR_200, Fundus Dataset, RFMiD, HRF, ARIA".
    Da tai thu va MO ANH THAT (394/400 anh lop "amd") -- PHAT HIEN QUAN
    TRONG: 237/394 anh (60%) co ten file dang "<so>_left.jpg" / "<so>_
    right.jpg" -- DUNG Y HET quy uoc dat ten cua chinh ODIR-5K (patient ID
    + mat trai/phai). Day la dau hieu RAT RO RANG cac anh nay duoc lay
    THANG TU ODIR-5K (1 trong 6 nguon duoc liet ke).
    => RUI RO DATA LEAKAGE THAT: model multidisease hien tai DA TUNG train
       tren toan bo tap train ODIR-5K (bao gom ca cac anh gan nhan "amd").
       Neu dung AMDNet23 de "kiem chung" multidisease hoac de train model
       AMD moi, RAT CO THE mot phan anh trung/gan giong anh da nam trong
       tap train cu -- ket qua do duoc se BI THOI PHONG (model "nhin quen"
       anh, khong phai thuc su tong quat hoa tot). Day la loi tuong tu nhu
       viec khong duoc danh gia model tren chinh tap da dung de train.
       CHUA XAC MINH DUOC ty le trung lap chinh xac (khong con giu manifest
       ODIR-5K goc de doi chieu tung ID) -- chi canh bao nguy co, khong
       khang dinh % trung lap cu the.
