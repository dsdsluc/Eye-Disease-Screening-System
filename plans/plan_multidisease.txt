3.2 Tao inference/multidisease_engine.py (mo phong inference/quality_
    engine.py -- KHONG dung ensemble/vmap nhu DR vi day chi la 1 backbone
    don, khong co fold):
      - MultiDiseaseEngine.__init__(): goi load_multidisease_model() 1 lan
        luc startup (giu am trong VRAM, giong triet ly muc 2.3 cua
        archetechture.txt), ap dung CUNG load_and_crop +
        preprocess_for_backbone(backbone_name) de dam bao khop phan phoi
        du lieu voi luc train (scripts/train_multidisease.py dung dung 2
        ham nay).
      - .predict(image_path) -> softmax(logits) tren 8 lop, tra ve:
          {
            "top_label": str,
            "top_prob": float,
            "top3": [{"label": str, "prob": float}, ...],
            "probs": {label: prob, ...},   # ca 8 lop, cho UI ve bieu do
          }
      - KHONG suy dien "co benh/khong co benh" nhi phan tu top_label --
        chi tra ve phan phoi xac suat, de web/ tu quyet dinh cach hien thi
        (tranh cung mot logic gate/blocked nhu quality vi day khong phai
        gatekeeper).

3.3 Sua inference/main.py:
      - lifespan: doi asyncio.gather(...) tu 2 thanh 3 phan tu (them
        asyncio.to_thread(MultiDiseaseEngine)) -- giu nguyen nguyen tac
        "song song, khong cong don thoi gian khoi dong" da ap dung cho
        quality_engine.
      - Endpoint moi: POST /multidisease-screen (multipart file) ->

        model_version, is_experimental}. model_version/is_experimental

        khi doi model, khong hardcode string canh bao trong web/.
      - KHONG dong bo hoa (gop) voi /predict nhu quality-check dang lam --
        day la endpoint doc lap, web/ goi rieng khi nguoi dung yeu cau,
        khong goi tu dong trong route.ts cua /predict.
