1. KIEN TRUC HIEN TAI (tom tat nhanh, de audit muc 2 co ngu canh)
----------------------------------------------------------
1 process FastAPI duy nhat (inference/main.py), 6 engine deu duoc load 1
lan trong lifespan (asyncio.gather, song song) va giu resident trong VRAM
suot vong doi process:
    /predict, /explain      -> InferenceEngine (DR, 20 model: 4 backbone x
                               5 fold, vmap-batched-forward, fp16)
    /quality-check          -> QualityEngine (QuickQual, DenseNet121 dong
                               bang + SVM sklearn)
    /multidisease-screen    -> MultiDiseaseEngine (1 backbone, 8-lop)
    /glaucoma-screen        -> GlaucomaEngine (1 backbone nhi phan + model
                               dinh vi dia thi giac rieng cua fundus_image_
                               toolbox)
    /amd-screen             -> AmdEngine (1 backbone nhi phan + Gamma/CLAHE)
    /cataract-screen        -> CataractEngine (1 backbone nhi phan + YuNet
                               face detector cv2.FaceDetectorYN cho auto-
                               crop mat, KHONG dung fundus preprocessing)

VRAM da do thuc te (6 engine cung luc): ~4.3-4.4GB / 8.15GB -- con du.

3. DE XUAT HUONG NANG CAP PERFORMANCE/LATENCY
----------------------------------------------------------
3.1 UU TIEN CAO: boc try/except quanh MOI cuoc goi engine.predict() trong
    inference/main.py, tra ve HTTPException(400, "Khong doc duoc anh, vui
    long kiem tra dinh dang file") thay vi de rot xuong 500 mac dinh. Day
    KHONG phai toi uu latency, nhung la "on dinh" (audit yeu cau ca 2) --
    ưu tiên truoc performance vi anh hung truc tiep den trai nghiem loi
    that (web/ dang nhan 500 vo nghia thay vi thong bao huu ich).

3.2 GIAM LATENCY -- /explain (Grad-CAM, 1.97s, cham nhat):
    Grad-CAM hien chay lai TOAN BO 4 backbone (forward + backward) rieng
    biet voi /predict -- neu web/ luon goi ca 2 (/predict roi /explain) cho
    cung 1 anh, co the gop thanh 1 endpoint tra ca grade LAN heatmap trong
    1 lan tien xu ly + 1 lan forward (backward chi them cho phan CAM), tiet
    kiem duoc 1 lan tien xu ly anh + 1 lan forward DR full (~0.7-0.8s uoc
    tinh dua tren chenh lech do duoc voi /predict). Can xac nhan truoc voi
    web/ xem 2 endpoint co luon di cung nhau khong roi moi gop.

3.3 GIAM VRAM/thoi gian khoi dong -- LAZY-LOAD cho cac engine it dung:
    Ca 6 model deu load NGAY LUC KHOI DONG (kien truc "always warm" hop ly
    cho DR vi la tinh nang chinh, dung lien tuc) -- nhung glaucoma/AMD/
    cataract la tinh nang PHU, nguoi dung chu dong kich hoat, tan suat goi
    thap hon nhieu so /predict. Co the doi sang tai LUOI (load lan dau tien
    goi den, cache lai cho cac lan sau) cho 3 engine nay -- danh doi: lan
    goi DAU TIEN cua moi engine se cham hon (them thoi gian load model), bu
    lai giam VRAM/thoi gian khoi dong tong the va it tai nguyen hon cho
    engine it dung. CAN CAN NHAC: hien VRAM con du (4.3/8.15GB), day la toi
    uu "phong ngua" hon la "can gap" luc nay -- uu tien thap hon 3.1/3.2.

3.4 GIAM SAT thay vi "sua ngay" cho muc 2.1 (crash flaky):
    Vi khong tai hien duoc on dinh, de xuat: (a) bat log co cau truc hon
    cho tung request (vd ghi lai ten file/kich thuoc truoc khi xu ly) de
    neu crash lai xay ra co du lieu dieu tra thay vi mat trang hoan toan
    nhu lan nay; (b) can nhac chay uvicorn voi tien trinh giam sat tu khoi
    dong lai (vd --reload khong hop cho production, nhung 1 process manager
    ben ngoai nhu supervisor/pm2/systemd voi auto-restart-on-crash se giam
    thiet hai neu loi flaky nay tai dien that trong luc dung thuc te) --
    hien tai neu server sap, TOAN BO 6 tinh nang (ke ca DR chinh) deu mat
    cho toi khi co nguoi thu cong khoi dong lai.

3.5 (Lien quan cau hoi da thao luan truoc, chua lam) Gatekeeper kiem tra
    modality dau vao (vd CLIP zero-shot, da thao luan o 1 phien truoc) --
