9.2 "San bang" do phan giai giua 2 lop TRUOC KHI dua vao model -- ky thuat

    Thay vi loai anh nho (mat du lieu) hay chi resize thang len 224 (giu
    nguyen loi the do net cua lop Cataract), ap dung cho MOI ANH (ca 2
    lop) 1 buoc "ep tran chi tiet chung":
      (1) Chon 1 nguong SHORT_SIDE_CAP (vd 80-100px -- CAN thu nghiem +
          xem anh mau truc quan o muc 9.3 truoc khi chon so cuoi cung,
          KHONG chon cung 54px cua lop Normal vi qua nho se xoa luon dau
          hieu duc thuy tinh the that, cung KHONG de nguyen 248px cua lop
          Cataract vi giu nguyen loi the do net).
      (2) Voi MOI anh: resize XUONG SHORT_SIDE_CAP truoc (cv2.resize,

          248px) LAN anh Normal (von 54px) DEU bi "tran" o cung 1 muc chi
          tiet toi da SAU buoc nay -- xoa bo tin hieu "do net con lai" ma

          tiet nho, nen van song sot qua buoc "ep tran" nay).
      (3) Ap dung buoc nay cho CA train LAN val (khong chi train) -- neu

9.7 CAP NHAT SAU KHI TRA CUU THEM (2026-09-02): CHON NGUONG SHORT_SIDE_CAP
    BANG CONG THUC, KHONG DOAN SO
    Da tra cuu them ve "shortcut learning tu confound (do phan giai/thiet
    bi chup) trong hoc sau y te" (Zech et al., cac bai tong quan "Shortcuts
    Causing Bias in Radiology AI") -- xac nhan huong "data-centric
    preprocessing / lam khop chat luong giua cac nhom" (muc 9.2) DUNG
    huong voi cac ky thuat da duoc cong bo (khong co 1 "cong thuc chuan"
    duy nhat duoc dat ten rieng cho truong hop nay, nhung day la ung dung
    hop ly cua nhom ky thuat "disparate impact removal" / "quality
    matching" da duoc nhac trong cac bai tong quan). CAC ky thuat hoc
    thuat manh hon (distillation tu teacher khong-thien-vi, toi uu LRP
